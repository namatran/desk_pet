How it would go together

  1. LCD screws to standoffs behind the window. The pot for contrast sits on the breadboard.
  2. The encoder shaft pokes through the face and its nut clamps it in place.
  3. Nano lies on the floor with its USB port at the back opening, so you can reflash it without opening the case.
  4. Print the shell and a flat base plate separately. The base screws on with M3 screws so you can get back inside.
  5. Both printers you're looking at have room for this many times over.
